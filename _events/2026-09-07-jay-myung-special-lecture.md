## 행사 소개

적응적 실험에서 실시간으로 설계를 조정하여 정보 획득량을 높이고 필요한 측정 횟수를 줄이는 방법을 살펴봅니다. 베이지안 방법론인 Adaptive Design Optimization(ADO)과 최근 확장을 통해 행동과학 연구의 측정과 추론을 효율화하는 접근을 소개합니다.

강의는 영어로, 질의응답과 토론은 영어와 한국어로 진행하도록 안내되었습니다.

## Abstract

<div lang="en" markdown="1">

Accurate and efficient measurement is fundamental to empirical research. Adaptive statistical methods can improve inference by increasing the informativeness of data and enhancing the efficiency of data collection, thereby accelerating the accumulation of scientific knowledge. In recent years, researchers have shown growing interest in adaptive experiments that maximize information gain while minimizing the number of measurements required. In adaptive experiments, design decisions are made and implemented online in real time, with minimal intervention from experimenters once the study begins. This talk introduces a practical Bayesian methodology, known as Adaptive Design Optimization (ADO), along with its recent extensions. ADO enables scientists to conduct ‘‘smart’’ autonomous experiments that are both highly informative and efficient, ultimately accelerating scientific discovery in the behavioral sciences and beyond.

</div>
