## 행사 소개

AI가 제공하는 설명이 통계적 추론에 미치는 영향과 구조화된 AI 리터러시 교육의 효과를 살펴봅니다. 도구의 성능과 함께 영역 지식, AI 리터러시, 방향 설정과 판단·조정 능력이 학습과 연구에 갖는 의미를 논의합니다.

## Abstract

<div lang="en" markdown="1">

AI tools can reduce cognitive friction, but access to capable tools does not guarantee understanding. This talk examines two complementary questions: How can people learn with AI, and how can they learn about AI? First, in a preregistered research program on statistical reasoning, we evaluated 15 large language models from six providers on p-values, 95% confidence intervals, and cross-study comparisons, then tested AI-generated explanations in a randomized experiment. Explanations improved accuracy, an AI–human framing tended to support earlier correction, and performance at the one-month follow-up remained above the initial level. Second, in a five-week preregistered intervention, students received structured training, unstructured exposure, or no additional exposure. Structured training produced stronger gains in subjective AI literacy, trust, and subsequent AI use, but improvement in objective literacy was small and did not endure. Together, these findings show that capable models are not enough. Effective use also requires domain knowledge, AI literacy, and the meta-skills of direction, discernment, and calibration. The talk considers implications for psychology education, research practice, and human-in-the-loop learning systems, while addressing the limits of short-term training and directions for future work.

</div>
