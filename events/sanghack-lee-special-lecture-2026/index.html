행사 소개

심리학 연구에서 사건 간 인과관계의 지각, 개입 효과, 개인의 결과에 관한 질문이 요구하는 서로 다른 비교와 근거를 살펴봅니다. 이론을 구체적인 개입으로 옮기는 과정과 실험·관찰 자료의 역할, 평균 효과와 개인 수준의 결과 사이의 차이를 논의합니다.

Abstract

Psychology raises several kinds of causal questions: what makes one event appear to cause another, how an intervention changes experience or behavior, and what causal evidence can tell us about a particular person’s outcome. These questions share a vocabulary but call for different comparisons and kinds of evidence. Drawing on examples from psychological research, this talk explores how causal inference can help clarify what a study asks and what its findings support. We will consider how researchers turn theoretical ideas into concrete interventions, how experiments and observational data can inform causal comparisons, and why learning about an average effect may leave questions about individual outcomes unresolved. The aim is to connect familiar research problems with a way of thinking that makes the intervention, comparison, and scope of a causal claim explicit.

연사 소개

Sanghack Lee is an Associate Professor in the Graduate School of Data Science at Seoul National University, where he leads the Causality Lab. He develops mathematical foundations and algorithms for learning causal relationships, evaluating interventions, and making decisions from observational and experimental data. His recent work uses causal structure and counterfactual reasoning to guide representation learning, improve robustness in reinforcement learning, and reduce biases in language model training.
