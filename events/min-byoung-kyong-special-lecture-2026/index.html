행사 소개

Abstract

Decoding neural signals, understanding how the brain generates conscious perception and mental representations, and modulating brain function to restore or enhance cognition represent three complementary frontiers of modern cognitive neuroscience. This seminar will present our recent research spanning (i) From the Brain—advancing prefrontal EEG-based brain–machine interfaces (BMIs) for cognitive decoding by integrating deep-learning-based EEG decoding with a machine-to-brain interface (MBI) toward Brain-to-Brain communication; (ii) At the Brain—investigating the neural mechanisms underlying conscious perception, with a particular focus on thalamic function, using EEG, MEG, and fMRI; and (iii) Into the Brain—developing non-invasive neuromodulation technologies based on transcranial current stimulation (tCS) and transcranial focused ultrasound stimulation (tFUS) for personalized cognitive enhancement. Beyond these scientific advances, the seminar will conclude with reflections on my research journey from neurobiology to cognitive neuroscience, highlighting key experiences, challenges, and career decisions, while offering practical insights and encouragement to students and early-career researchers as they shape their own scientific careers.
